--- Advanced usage ---

CLIP_PROB_THRESHOLD = CLIP confidence less than this won't be trusted, default: 0.65
SSIM_THRESHOLD = SSIM similarity score that means video-frames are too similar and only one will be taken
    default: 0.9
COLOR_HIST_THRESHOLD = Color historgram similarity score that means video-frames are too similar and only
    one will be taken, default: 0.9
VIDEO_SECONDS_PER_FRAME = How many seconds to skip in a video to get the next frame, default: 4

YOLO_PROB_THRESHOLD = YOLO confidence less than this won't be trusted, default: 0.65
